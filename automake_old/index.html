What this is. Each network is an end-to-end transformer (8.77 million parameters) that writes every part of the wall itself: an item, then the part's four edges on 5 mm rulers, sixteen parts per pass until a pass writes nothing. The networks learned by imitating example walls and were never given framing or bricklaying rules; they read generic boxes (the wall, its openings, the parts already there), free-space boxes and a coarse coverage grid computed from them, and the brief.
